Scaffold

Privacy Policy

Your words. Your devices.

Scaffold gives your writing structure while keeping you in control of it. Scaffold was previously named Gradus; existing writing and privacy choices are preserved.

Your writing stays with you

Scaffold stores your projects, answers, drafts, source notes, assignment documents and settings on your device. When you enable iCloud Sync in Settings, Scaffold also stores project revisions and documents in your private iCloud database to synchronize devices using the same Apple account. The app has no account, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics. The Scaffold developer does not receive your writing or a copy of your library. Scaffold does not send your text to an AI service.

Backups and files you choose to share

Scaffold keeps local versioned backups and retains synchronized revisions in iCloud so earlier work and conflicting edits can be recovered. iCloud storage counts toward your Apple account storage allowance. You can choose a second backup folder or export a paper. If you choose iCloud Drive, another cloud provider, or an app for sharing a file, that service handles the copy under its own privacy terms. Your device's backup settings may also include Scaffold data. Scaffold does not operate a cloud storage service.

Optional dictation

When you start dictation in Scaffold, the app asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission. Scaffold requires on-device recognition and does not send the recording to a server. Audio is used for the active dictation session and is not saved as a recording by Scaffold; the words you keep become part of your writing. If on-device recognition is unavailable, you can keep typing. You can change these permissions in system settings.

Optional reminders and timers

Project reminders and session timers are optional. Scaffold schedules notifications locally on a device where you enable them; no notification server receives your writing. Notifications may show the project title according to your system's preview settings. Project planning preferences are saved with your library and follow your iCloud Sync choice. Notification permission is controlled separately on each device. Scaffold does not access Apple Reminders.

Keeping and removing your work

Your local library and version history stay on your device until you remove them. Synchronized revisions also remain in your private iCloud database. Turning off iCloud Sync pauses transfers without removing local writing or existing cloud history. Signing out pauses sync without deleting local writing; another Apple account does not automatically receive it. Moving a project to Recently Deleted does not erase its saved versions. To remove all copies, remove Scaffold's iCloud data through Apple's iCloud storage settings as well as local app data, backup files, exported files and second-copy backups. Removing cloud data pauses this library's synchronization to protect its remaining local copies. Cloud providers and device backups may retain their own copies according to your settings. The developer cannot retrieve or delete your private iCloud library on your behalf.

TestFlight and feedback

If you use the beta through TestFlight, Apple handles installation and automatically collects crash logs and usage data that are made available to the developer under Apple's TestFlight terms. Feedback you choose to submit can include your comments, contact details and screenshots. Include writing or personal details only if you want to share them for troubleshooting. This is separate from Scaffold's local library and backups.

About TestFlight privacy

Support and privacy questions

For support or privacy questions, email xsf@speedpost.net. Include your device, app version and a description of the issue. Share writing or assignment documents only if you choose to. During the beta, you can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight. General bug reports can go to the public issue tracker. Do not post your writing, assignment documents or personal information there.
